# MirrorLane: practise driving on the other side of the road

MirrorLane is an iOS app that helps licensed drivers prepare for a trip where the traffic runs on the opposite side. It rebuilds the habits that catch travellers out before they arrive, using a short daily plan paced to the time left before the trip. Every drill mirrors to the switch you are making, left-side to right or right to left.

## What it does

MirrorLane rehearses the moments that go wrong on quiet roads, where there is no car in front to copy:

- Which way to look first
- Which lane to finish a turn in
- Joining empty roads from car parks and driveways
- Roundabouts that flow the other way
- Where everything sits in a mirrored car

Tell the app where you normally drive and where you are going, set your trip date, and get a short session each day. With weeks to go, you run the full plan. Flying tomorrow, the Airport Cram fits the essentials into one session before boarding.

## Details
