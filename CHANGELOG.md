## [1.0] - 2026-07-30

First public release.

### Added

- Daily practice plan that mirrors to your switch, left-side to right or right to left
- Drills for the moments that catch travellers out: which way to look first, which lane to finish a turn in, joining empty roads, roundabouts that flow the other way, and the layout of a mirrored car
- Airport Cram, a single condensed session for last-minute trips
- Trip date setup with sessions paced to the time available
- Offline use on iPhone, iOS 17 or later
